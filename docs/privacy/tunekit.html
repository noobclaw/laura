TuneBench Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-05

TuneBench does not collect any data.

The app runs entirely on your device: no account is required, it contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs, it requests no network permission, and it sends nothing to us or to any third-party server. Your practice log is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

The microphone is used only while the tuner or the play-and-check mode is open, to measure pitch in real time. Audio is analysed in memory only — never recorded, saved or uploaded — and the microphone is switched off as soon as you leave those screens or the app goes to the background.

In-app purchases (if any) are processed by the official Apple App Store / Google Play billing systems; we never see or store any of your payment information.
