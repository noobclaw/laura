离线老照片修复 PhotoLift 隐私政策

生效日期:2026-09-05

离线老照片修复(PhotoLift)不收集任何数据。

本应用完全在你的设备本地运行:不需要账号、不含广告与第三方分析 SDK、不申请网络权限,不向我们或任何第三方服务器发送任何信息。AI 放大与降噪由内置在应用里的模型在你的手机上完成,照片从不上传。

你选择的照片及其修复结果只保存在本应用的私有目录中;只有当你点击「保存到相册」时,结果才会写入系统相册(iOS 上会请求「添加照片」权限,应用不读取相册内容)。删除应用即随之删除应用内的全部数据。

应用内购买由 Apple App Store / Google Play 官方结算系统处理,我们不经手、也无法访问你的任何支付信息。

PhotoLift Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-05

PhotoLift does not collect any data.

The app runs entirely on your device: no account is required, it contains no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs, it requests no network permission, and it sends nothing to us or to any third-party server. AI upscaling and denoising are performed by a model bundled inside the app, on your phone; photos are never uploaded.

The photos you pick and their enhanced results are stored only in the app's private folder. A result is written to your system photo library only when you tap "Save to Photos" (on iOS this asks for add-only access; the app never reads your library). Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

In-app purchases are processed by the official Apple App Store / Google Play billing systems; we never see or store any of your payment information.
