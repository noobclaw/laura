## Description

Light one lantern in every row, every column and every colour. Lanterns never touch, not even at a corner. That is the whole rule, and it is enough for hundreds of puzzles.

ONE ANSWER, NO GUESSING
Every board has exactly one solution and can be solved by reasoning alone. If you are stuck, a hint shows the next step and tells you why it is true.

FROM 5×5 TO 10×10
300 levels that grow from small warm-up boards to full 10×10 grids, plus a new daily puzzle every day. After level 300 the boards keep coming.

A GARDEN, NOT A SPREADSHEET
The board sits in a moonlit 3D garden. Lanterns glow when you place them, tiles ripple when you get one right, and the garden lights up when you finish.

MIND THE KEEPER
While you think, rocks pile up on the little keeper below the board. Each new lantern knocks some away. Let the pile get too high and the round ends, so a slow start still needs a strong finish.

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Auto-mark crosses out tiles a lantern rules out
• Undo, restart, and three free hints on every board
• Music and sound effects with separate switches
• English, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and Traditional Chinese
• Plays offline. No account, no adverts, no subscription

IN-APP PURCHASES
Extra hints and rock blasts can be bought alone or in packs. They are optional: every level can be finished without them.

## App Review notes

No account or sign-in is needed. In-app purchases: on the home screen tap "Shop" to see all five consumable items. They can also be reached in a level: the Hint button opens the shop when no hints are left, and when rocks fill the bar the "Keep going" button offers the Keeper's Rescue pack. The game is a local web build shown in a WKWebView; it makes no network requests other than StoreKit.
