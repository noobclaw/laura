Lantern Garden: Queens Puzzle

Help and contact

Lantern Garden is a logic puzzle: light one lantern in every row, every column and every colour, and never let two lanterns touch.

How do I play?

Tap a tile once to mark it empty and twice to light a lantern. Each row, each column and each coloured area holds exactly one lantern, and lanterns may not touch, not even at a corner. Every level has one answer and can be solved without guessing.

What are the rocks?

Rocks pile up on the keeper's plank while you think. Each new lantern you place takes some away. If the pile gets too high the round ends; you can try the same board again for free, or carry on by using a rock blast.

What can I buy?

Hints and rock blasts, alone or in packs. They are used up as you play. There is no subscription and there are no adverts. The game can be finished without buying anything.

I paid but did not get my items

Close the game completely and open it again; a purchase that was interrupted is finished on the next start. If the items are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, or email us and we will walk you through it.

Do I keep my items on a new phone?

Progress and items are stored on the device and are not synced. Items that have not been used do not move to another device or come back after the game is deleted.

Does the game need the internet?

No. It only contacts the App Store when you open the shop or buy something.
