**Description**
```
Draftbook is a writing app for long work — novels, non-fiction, scripts — built for the phone and tablet you actually carry.

STRUCTURE, NOT ONE LONG PAGE
A book is a project. A project has chapters. A chapter has scenes. Drag scenes to reorder them, move them between chapters, or spread every scene across one outline board and rearrange the whole book in a minute.

WRITE WITHOUT FIGHTING THE APP
The formatting bar above the keyboard is always there and always works. No modal dialogs while you write. Open the app and the cursor is back where you stopped — two taps, every time.

YOUR DRAFT, NOT YOUR CLOUD ACCOUNT
Everything is stored on your device first. Every scene keeps its own version history, so a bad afternoon is one tap away from being undone. Turn on sync and your projects move between your phone, your tablet and your other devices through our own service — no third-party storage subscription required, and nothing to configure.

WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL AND NO ACCOUNT
Writing, outlining, word counts, version history and export all run entirely offline. An account is only needed if you want your drafts on more than one device. If our servers are down, nothing about your writing changes.

EXPORT WHEN YOU'RE DONE
Send the whole manuscript out as Markdown, plain text or DOCX, with your chapter titles in place.

FREE
- One project, unlimited words
- Chapters, scenes and the outline board
- Word-count goals and streaks
- Local version history
- Markdown and TXT export

PRO — ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
- Unlimited projects
- Sync across all your devices
- 30 days of cloud version history
- DOCX export
- Research board for notes, images and character cards

One purchase. Every device you own. No monthly fee.
```

## 简体中文（不发中国大陆区，仍须填）

**名称**

```
Draftbook 写长篇
```

**副标题**
```
章节 · 大纲 · 多设备同步
```

**描述**
```
Draftbook 是给长篇写作用的 —— 小说、长稿、剧本 —— 为你随身带的手机和平板做的。

有结构，不是一整页
一本书是一个项目，项目分章，章分场景。拖动场景就能重排，也能跨章移动；打开大纲板，整本书的场景一屏铺开，一分钟就能重新排好顺序。

写的时候不跟 app 较劲
键盘上方的工具条随时都在、随时能点。写作中不弹任何窗。打开 app，光标就回到上次停笔的地方 —— 两次点击，每次都是。

稿子先存在你自己手机里
所有内容优先存本地。每个场景各自留版本历史，写砸的那个下午，一键就能撤回。打开同步后，项目在你的手机、平板和其他设备之间自己走 —— 不需要另外买第三方网盘，也没有任何要配置的东西。

没网、没账号，照样能写
写作、大纲、字数统计、版本历史、导出，全部离线可用。只有想在多台设备上写才需要账号。我们的服务器就算挂了，你的写作体验一点不变。

写完导得出去
整本稿子可以导出为 Markdown、纯文本或 DOCX，章节标题都在。

免费包含
- 1 个项目，字数不限
- 章节、场景与大纲板
- 字数目标与连续天数
- 本地版本历史
- Markdown / TXT 导出

Pro —— 一次买断，不订阅
- 项目数量不限
- 全设备同步
- 30 天云端版本历史
- DOCX 导出
- 资料板：笔记、图片、人物卡

买一次，你所有的设备都能用。没有月费。
```

**关键词**
```
长篇,小说,写作,码字,大纲,章节,场景,书稿,作者,同步,离线,导出
```
