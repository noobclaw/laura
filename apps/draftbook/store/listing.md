**Description**
```
Draftbook is a writing app for long work — novels, non-fiction, scripts — built for the phone and tablet you actually carry.

STRUCTURE, NOT ONE LONG PAGE
A book is a project. A project has chapters. A chapter has scenes. Drag scenes to reorder them, move them between chapters, and see the whole book as one outline you can rearrange in a minute.

WRITE WITHOUT FIGHTING THE APP
The formatting bar above the keyboard is always there and always works. No dialogs interrupt your writing. Open the app and the cursor is back where you stopped — two taps, every time.

NOTHING YOU WROTE IS EVER LOST
Every scene keeps its last 20 versions on the device. One is taken when you first change the text and another when you leave, so a bad afternoon is a couple of taps from being undone — and restoring is itself undoable, because the text it replaces is saved first.

NO ACCOUNT, NO CLOUD, NO NETWORK
Draftbook ships without permission to use the internet at all. Your manuscript is stored on this device — and nowhere but your own device backup. Your work only leaves the app when you export it yourself.

WORD GOALS THAT MEAN SOMETHING
A daily goal, a streak, and the last two weeks as a chart. Words cut count as cut, so the number is the truth rather than a pat on the head.

EXPORT WHEN YOU'RE READY
Send the whole manuscript out as Markdown or plain text, with your chapter titles in place. Pro adds Word (.docx) with a page break per chapter and real heading styles.

FREE
- One book, unlimited words, chapters and scenes
- The draggable outline
- Daily word goal and streak
- Version history for every scene
- Markdown and TXT export

PRO — ONE PAYMENT, NO SUBSCRIPTION
- Unlimited books
- Word (.docx) export

One purchase, no account, no monthly fee.
```

## 简体中文(不发中国大陆区,仍须填)

**名称**
```
Draftbook 写长篇
```
> ⚠️ 名称里刻意不含「写作」「码字」等中区在位者可能逐字同名的词(09-13 `倒数日` 撞上 270 万条评价免费龙头的教训)。

**副标题**
```
章节 · 场景 · 大纲重排
```

**描述**
```
Draftbook 是给长篇写作用的 —— 小说、长稿、剧本 —— 为你随身带的手机和平板做的。

有结构,不是一整页
一本书是一个项目,项目分章,章分场景。拖动场景就能重排,也能跨章移动;整本书的场景一屏铺开,一分钟就能重新排好顺序。

写的时候不跟 app 较劲
键盘上方的工具条随时都在、随时能点。写作中不弹任何窗。打开 app,光标就回到上次停笔的地方 —— 两次点击,每次都是。

写过的字不会丢
每个场景各自留最近 20 个版本,存在本机。开始改动时留一版、离开编辑器再留一版;写砸的那个下午,两三下就能撤回 —— 而且「恢复」本身也能再撤回,因为被替换掉的那一版会先被存起来。

没有账号,没有云,连网络权限都没有
Draftbook 根本没有申请联网权限。稿子只存在这台设备上(以及你自己的系统备份里),不去别的地方。只有你自己点导出,它才会出去。

字数目标是真的
每日目标、连续天数,以及最近两周的柱状图。删掉的字会照实扣回去 —— 这个数字是实话,不是哄你开心。

写完导得出去
整本稿子可以导出为 Markdown 或纯文本,章节标题都在。Pro 再加一个 Word(.docx):按章分页,标题带大纲级别。

免费包含
- 1 本书,字数、章数、场景数都不限
- 可拖拽的大纲
- 每日字数目标与连续天数
- 每个场景的版本历史
- Markdown / TXT 导出

Pro —— 一次买断,不订阅
- 项目数量不限
- Word(.docx)导出

买一次就好,没有账号,也没有月费。
```
