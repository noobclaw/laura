2. Purpose and audience
AstroPile is for hobby astrophotographers who shoot the night sky with a phone. A single phone exposure of the stars is noisy. AstroPile aligns a burst of 4–32 photos of the same patch of sky star-by-star and averages them into one cleaner image. What sets it apart is that alignment is visible: for every frame it reports how many stars were detected, how many were matched, the residual error in pixels and a quality score, and it explains why a frame failed (too few stars, no matching star pattern, residual too large, different image size). The user can untick bad frames and stack again.

3. How to access the main features
No login or credentials are needed. Any set of 4 or more photos of the same star field works; photos taken seconds apart on a tripod with the built-in Camera app are ideal. Steps: open the app → "Choose photos" → select the burst → "Stack". When it finishes, open the alignment report, optionally untick frames and re-stack, adjust black point and brightness, then save. The free tier stacks up to 6 frames; the one-time "Pro" in-app purchase raises this to 32 frames and enables the advanced stacking modes.

4. External services
None. All processing (star detection, registration, stacking, stretching) runs on the device in the app's own code. The app does not request network access and uses no third-party data providers, authentication services, analytics, advertising or AI services. The only payment processor is Apple In-App Purchase (one non-consumable product).

5. Regional differences
None. The app functions identically in every region. The interface is available in English and Simplified Chinese and follows the language the user selects.

6. Regulated industries / third-party material
Not applicable. The app does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party content; it only processes photos the user selects from their own library.
