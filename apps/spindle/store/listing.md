## Description

Spindle plays the music you keep on your NAS or server — and makes it look like a record shelf again.

ADD YOUR SERVER IN A MINUTE
• WebDAV: works with Synology (WebDAV Server), QNAP, Nextcloud, rclone and most home servers.
• Any folder in the Files app: this iPhone, iCloud Drive, or an SMB server you connected in Files.
• Not ready yet? A demo shelf with eight short pieces is there from the first launch.

YOUR LIBRARY, INSTANTLY
• Albums appear as soon as the folders are listed, then tags and artwork fill in quietly in the background.
• Artwork is kept on your iPhone, so it’s still there after a restart or a network change.
• Search every source at once: songs, albums, artists.

PLAYBACK THAT KEEPS GOING
• Picks up again after calls and notifications, and quietly reconnects a stalled stream at the same spot.
• Lock screen and Control Center controls, AirPlay, shuffle and repeat, a queue you can reorder.
• Pauses when you unplug your headphones.

TAKE IT WITH YOU
• Download albums and playlists for offline listening, one after another until done.
• Playlists that mix songs from different servers.

SHARE YOUR SHELF
• A record-shelf card or a “listening now” card — without server names or addresses.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. Spindle collects no data. Passwords stay in your iPhone’s Keychain; music goes straight from your server to you.

SPINDLE PRO — ONE PAYMENT, NOT A SUBSCRIPTION
Free: one server or folder plus the demo shelf, two playlists, 20 offline songs.
Pro: unlimited sources, playlists and offline songs, plus a sleep timer with a gentle fade. Shared with your family.

Spindle plays MP3, AAC/M4A, ALAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF. Synology and QNAP are trademarks of their owners; Spindle is not affiliated with them.

## Review notes (App Review)
No account or server is needed to test: on first launch the Demo shelf contains eight short pieces generated by the app (no third-party content). Every playback feature (queue, shuffle, lock screen, offline download, playlists, share cards) works with it. To test a server, any WebDAV address can be added under Shelf › Sources › Add a Source. Spindle Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase submitted with this version; the paywall opens from Settings › Spindle Pro or when adding a second source.
