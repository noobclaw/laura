**长描述**
图片万能箱(PicWorks)把最常用的六件图片小工具装进一个纯离线的 app:压缩、缩放、格式转换、裁剪旋转、去除元数据、加水印。每个工具都支持批量,处理全部在你的手机上完成——不申请网络权限,没有账号,没有广告。

■ 六个工具
• 压缩:按画质,或直接指定「每张不超过 500 KB / 2 MB」,先降画质再缩尺寸,结果一目了然
• 缩放:按像素、百分比或最长边,默认不把小图拉大;可选保持比例或拉伸
• 格式转换:JPEG、PNG、WebP 互转,读取 HEIC;透明图转 JPEG 自动填白

• 水印:文字水印,九宫格定位或整图斜向平铺,大小、透明度、颜色、投影可调,所见即所得

• 批量:一次选多张,一个进度条跑完,逐张显示前后体积与尺寸,拖动分割线对比画质
• 一次买断:免费版六个工具全功能,每次最多 3 张;Pro 一次付费解锁不限张数、WebP 导出、水印预设和记住设置
• 中文优先,支持深色模式

## English

**Title (≤30 chars)**
PicWorks: Image Toolbox

**Short description (≤80 chars)**
Compress, resize, convert, crop, strip EXIF/GPS, watermark. Offline, in batches.

**Full description**
PicWorks puts the six image tools you actually use into one fully offline app: compress, resize, convert, crop & rotate, strip metadata, and watermark. Every tool works in batches, and everything happens on your phone — no network permission, no account, no ads.

■ Six tools
• Compress: by quality, or set a target like "under 500 KB / 2 MB" — quality first, then size, with before/after numbers for each picture
• Resize: by pixels, percent or longest side; never upscales unless you ask; keep aspect or stretch
• Convert: JPEG, PNG and WebP in any direction; reads HEIC; transparent images get a white background when going to JPEG
• Crop & Rotate: free, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16 and more (13 presets), 90° rotation and flips; phone photos are put upright before cropping
• Strip Metadata: see the device, time, software and GPS coordinates hidden in each picture, then remove all of it losslessly — not a single pixel is re-encoded
• Watermark: text marks placed on a 3×3 grid or tiled diagonally across the picture; size, opacity, colour and shadow, with a live preview that matches the export

■ Why PicWorks
• Six-in-one: no separate app (and separate subscription) for compressing, stripping EXIF and watermarking
• Truly offline: the app requests no network permission, so your pictures never leave your phone
• Batch: pick many, watch one progress bar, then compare every result with a draggable before/after slider
• Buy once: the free version runs every tool in full on up to 3 pictures per run; Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited batches, WebP export, watermark presets and remembered settings
• Dark mode, English and Chinese

■ Who it's for
Anyone squeezing photos under a forum, email or form limit, anyone removing location data before sharing, creators signing their work in bulk, and everyone converting between HEIC, PNG and WebP.

Free: all six tools, up to 3 pictures per run. Pro (one-time): unlimited batch size, WebP export, saved watermark presets, and every tool remembers your last settings.
