## 中文(Play 中文 / 后续 App Store 中区)

**标题**(30 字内):回声笔记 - 离线语音转文字备忘
**短描述**(80 字内):点一下就说,文字边说边出。全程离线、不留录音——你的声音不出这台手机。

**长描述**:

想法总在走路、开车、排队时冒出来:打字来不及,录音又搜不到。

回声笔记:点一下话筒开始说,文字**边说边出现**;说完就是一条可搜索的文字笔记。没有账号,没有订阅,**没有网络权限**——你的声音不出这台手机。

• **真离线听写**:使用你手机系统内置的「设备端语音识别」,不下载模型、不联网,飞行模式照样用
• **连录音都不留**:应用不保存任何音频文件——没有录音,也就没有录音会泄露
• **边说边出字**:一边说一边看到文字,长段落自动分句、自动补标点
• **全文搜索**:说过的每一句话都搜得到
• **自由编辑与分享**:识别错的地方直接改,一键分享或导出
• **零网络权限**:应用未申请联网权限,你可以在系统设置里自己验证
• **一次买断**:免费 30 条笔记,一次付费永久解锁无限笔记 + 全量导出,拒绝订阅

**使用要求**:Android 12 及以上,且系统中已安装「设备端语音识别」语言包(在「系统设置 → 系统 → 语言与输入法 → 设备端语音识别」下载,通常中英文都可下载)。若系统没有该能力,应用会明确提示——我们绝不会为了"能用"把你的声音传到云端。

适合:会议速记、灵感捕捉、口述草稿,以及在意隐私的你。

**关键词**:语音备忘,语音转文字,离线,不联网,语音笔记,速记,听写,隐私,买断

## English (Play EN / App Store US later)

**Title** (30 chars): EchoJot — Offline Voice Notes
**Short description** (80 chars): Tap and talk; text appears as you speak. Fully offline, no recordings kept.

**Description**:

Ideas strike while walking, driving, or waiting in line. Typing is too slow, and voice memos can't be searched.

EchoJot: tap the mic and start talking — **the text appears as you speak**, and you end up with a searchable note. No account. No subscription. **No network permission at all** — your voice never leaves this phone.

• **Genuinely offline dictation** — uses your phone's built-in on-device speech recognition. No model download, no internet, works in airplane mode
• **No audio is kept** — the app stores no recordings at all. Nothing to leak
• **Live text** — see words as you speak; long passages are split into sentences and punctuated for you
• **Full-text search** across everything you've ever said
• **Edit & share** — fix anything misheard, then share or export
• **Zero network permission** — verify it yourself in system settings
• **Pay once** — 30 free notes; one unlock for unlimited notes and full export. Never a subscription

**Requirements**: Android 12+ with an on-device speech recognition language pack installed (Settings → System → Languages & input → On-device speech recognition). If your system has no on-device recognition, the app tells you plainly — it will never send your voice to a server just to appear to work.

For meetings, ideas, spoken drafts, and anyone who cares about privacy.

**Keywords**: voice notes, offline, on-device dictation, speech to text, voice memo, private, no subscription, no internet
