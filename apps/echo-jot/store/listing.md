## 中文(Play 中文 / 后续 App Store 中区)

**标题**(30 字内):回声笔记 - 离线语音转文字备忘
**短描述**(80 字内):点一下就说,文字边说边出。全程离线、不留录音——你的声音不出这台手机。

**长描述**:

想法总在走路、开车、排队时冒出来:打字来不及,录音又搜不到。

回声笔记:点一下话筒开始说,文字**边说边出现**;说完就是一条可搜索的文字笔记。没有账号,没有订阅,**没有网络权限**——你的声音不出这台手机。

• **真离线听写,两种引擎任选**:系统内置的「设备端语音识别」边说边出字;或应用自带的 Whisper 模型(安装时已包含,不下载)停止后转写,不依赖系统语音设置。都不联网,飞行模式照样用
• **不保留录音**:系统识别路径完全不写音频;Whisper 路径的录音只是转写期间的临时文件,转完即删——没有留下的录音,也就没有录音会泄露
• **边说边出字**(系统识别引擎):一边说一边看到文字,长段落自动分句、自动补标点;Whisper 引擎则在停止后整段转写、自带标点
• **全文搜索**:说过的每一句话都搜得到
• **自由编辑与分享**:识别错的地方直接改,一键分享或导出
• **零网络权限**:应用未申请联网权限,你可以在系统设置里自己验证
• **一次买断**:免费 30 条笔记,一次付费永久解锁无限笔记 + 全量导出,拒绝订阅

**使用要求**:Android 12 及以上,或 iOS 15.6 及以上。「系统识别」引擎需要系统装有对应语言的离线识别(Android 在「系统设置 → 系统 → 语言与输入法 → 设备端语音识别」下载语言包;iOS 由系统提供,含中文、英文、日文、韩文等);「Whisper 离线」引擎自带模型,任何机型开箱即用。若系统识别不可用,应用会明确提示并可一键改用 Whisper——我们绝不会为了"能用"把你的声音传到云端。

适合:会议速记、灵感捕捉、口述草稿,以及在意隐私的你。

**关键词**:语音备忘,语音转文字,离线,不联网,语音笔记,速记,听写,隐私,买断

## English (Play EN / App Store US later)

**Title** (30 chars): EchoJot — Offline Voice Notes
**Short description** (80 chars): Tap and talk; text appears as you speak. Fully offline, no recordings kept.

**Description**:

Ideas strike while walking, driving, or waiting in line. Typing is too slow, and voice memos can't be searched.

EchoJot: tap the mic and start talking — **the text appears as you speak**, and you end up with a searchable note. No account. No subscription. **No network permission at all** — your voice never leaves this phone.

• **Genuinely offline dictation, two engines** — your phone's built-in on-device speech recognition (text as you speak), or the Whisper model bundled inside the app (included at install, never downloaded) that transcribes after you stop and needs no system speech settings. Neither goes online; works in airplane mode
• **No recording is kept** — the system engine writes no audio at all; the Whisper engine holds the recording only as a temporary file while transcribing and deletes it right after. Nothing left to leak
• **Live text** (system engine) — see words as you speak; long passages are split into sentences and punctuated for you. The Whisper engine transcribes the whole take after you stop, punctuation included
• **Full-text search** across everything you've ever said
• **Edit & share** — fix anything misheard, then share or export
• **Zero network permission** — verify it yourself in system settings
• **Pay once** — 30 free notes; one unlock for unlimited notes and full export. Never a subscription

**Requirements**: Android 12+ or iOS 15.6+. The System engine needs the system's offline recognition for your language (Android: a pack from Settings → System → Languages & input → On-device speech recognition; iOS: provided by the system — Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and more). The Whisper engine ships its own model and works on any supported phone out of the box. If system recognition is unavailable, the app says so plainly and offers Whisper with one tap — it will never send your voice to a server just to appear to work.

For meetings, ideas, spoken drafts, and anyone who cares about privacy.

**Keywords**: voice notes, offline, on-device dictation, speech to text, voice memo, private, no subscription, no internet
