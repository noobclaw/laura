## English

### Title (≤30 chars)
AutoSnore: Offline Snore Log

### Short description (≤80 chars)
Record snoring all night, fully offline. No account, no upload, no network.

### Long description (≤4000 chars)
Find out if — and how much — you snore, without handing your bedroom audio to a
cloud server. AutoSnore listens through the microphone while you sleep and turns
the night into a simple morning report: how many times you snored, how loud, and
when. Everything happens on your phone.

WHY AUTOSNORE
Most snore trackers are subscriptions that upload or lock your nightly recordings
behind an account. AutoSnore is the opposite:
• Fully offline — the app ships without the INTERNET permission at all. Your
  sleep is one of the most private things there is; it should never leave your
  phone.
• No audio is even stored — AutoSnore measures loudness on-device in real time
  and keeps only the numbers, not a recording of your night.
• One-time purchase — no subscription, no ads. Buy Pro once and keep it.

YOUR MORNING REPORT
• Snore score (0–100) with a quiet / mild / moderate / loud band.
• Snore events — how many times, and how often per hour.
• Time spent snoring — the share of the night with noise.
• Loudest moment of the night.
• A loudness graph across the whole night, hour by hour.
• A rhythm check — rhythmic sounds are flagged as more likely real snoring;
  scattered ones as ambient noise.

HOW IT WORKS
Put your phone on the nightstand and keep it charging. AutoSnore keeps the screen
on and continuously measures how loud the room is. When the sound rises clearly
above the background for a moment, it logs a snore / noise event. Adjustable
sensitivity lets you catch faint sounds or only clear, loud snoring.

FREE VS PRO
Free: record every night and see the full morning report; the last 3 nights are
kept.
Pro (one-time $3.99): unlimited history, night-to-night trends, comparison, and
CSV export.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No network permission. No audio saved. Nothing is uploaded,
tracked, or shared — ever.

Please note: AutoSnore is a recording and self-observation tool, not a medical
device, and does not diagnose any condition. For persistent snoring or suspected
sleep apnea, please see a doctor.

### 完整描述（≤4000 字）
想知道自己到底打不打鼾、打得有多凶，又不想把整夜卧室录音交给云端服务器?鼾声记录
在你睡觉时通过麦克风聆听,把一整夜浓缩成一份简单的晨间报告:你打了多少次鼾、有多
响、都在什么时候。全部只在你的手机上完成。

为什么选鼾声记录
市面上多数打鼾 App 都是订阅制,还会上传你的整夜录音、或把它锁进账号里。鼾声记录
恰恰相反:
• 完全离线——应用出厂就不带任何 INTERNET 权限。睡眠是最私密的数据之一,它不该离开
  你的手机。
• 连录音都不保存——鼾声记录在设备端实时测量声音响度,只留下数字,不留下你整夜的
  录音文件。
• 一次买断——没有订阅、没有广告。Pro 买一次,永久使用。

你的晨间报告
• 鼾声评分(0–100),分安静 / 轻微 / 中等 / 响亮四档。
• 鼾声次数——一共几次、每小时几次。
• 鼾声占比——整夜有多少时间在出声。
• 全夜最响的时刻。
• 一条整夜响度曲线,一小时一小时看过去。
• 节律判断——规律出现的响声更可能是持续打鼾,零散的更像偶发环境噪音。

如何使用
把手机放在床头并接通电源。鼾声记录会保持屏幕常亮、持续测量房间的响度;当声音明显
高于背景并持续一小段时间,就记为一次「鼾声/响声」。灵敏度可调,既能捕捉轻微响声,
也能只记录明显的大声打鼾。

免费 vs Pro
免费:每晚都能记录并查看完整晨间报告,保留最近 3 晚。
Pro(一次性买断 ¥18 / $3.99):无限历史、跨夜趋势、两晚对比、CSV 导出。

隐私优先
无账号、无广告、无网络权限、不保存录音。绝不上传、不追踪、不分享。

请注意:鼾声记录是记录与自我观察工具,并非医疗设备,不用于诊断任何疾病。若长期打鼾
或怀疑睡眠呼吸暂停,请咨询医生。
