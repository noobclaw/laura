### 简短描述(≤80 字)
调音器、节拍器、和弦音阶练习三合一。弹一下就知道准不准,练了多久自动记着。离线、买断、无广告。

### 完整描述

**一个练琴时真正会打开的工具箱:调音、打拍、练和弦,还替你记着练了多久。**

**■ 调音器:指针稳、反应快**
弹一根弦,大表盘立刻指出偏高还是偏低,精确到音分;进入 ±5 音分的绿色区间就亮绿。半音模式给任何乐器调音;吉他、尤克里里、小提琴、贝斯预设把每根弦做成按钮,自动识别你正在弹的是哪根。标准音 A4 可在 430–450 Hz 之间调整。

**■ 节拍器:按采样排程,不飘**
节拍不是靠定时器「大概到点」,而是由音频引擎按采样精确排程,快到 300 也稳。2/4、3/4、4/4、6/8 拍号,四分、八分、三连音、十六分细分,第一拍重音,拍点跟着亮灯。打拍测速、长按微调。锁屏后继续走拍,来电时自动停止。

**■ 和弦与音阶:看得到,也弹得对**
近 50 种和弦、音阶与调式类型,12 个根音,每一个都标出组成音和音级,画在吉他 / 尤克里里指板或键盘上。吉他和尤克里里还给出自动推导的第一把位指法。
「弹奏检查」模式打开麦克风:逐根弦弹,它逐根弦确认对不对、偏了几音分;音阶就顺着弹一遍,它一个音一个音跟着打勾。
「随机训练」十题一轮:看音认和弦,看名字认音,连对有加分。

**■ 练习记录:练了多久、准不准**
每天在调音器、节拍器、练习里花的时间自动记账;调音时稳定读数里有多少落在 ±5 音分内,画成趋势;连续练习天数点亮火焰。每天满一分钟就算数。

**■ 完全离线,没有网络权限**
这个 App 根本没有联网权限,可以在系统里自己核对。麦克风只在调音和弹奏检查时打开,声音只在手机里即时分析,不录音、不上传;离开页面或切到后台立即关闭。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计 SDK。

## English (en-US)

### Title (≤30 chars)
TuneBench: Tuner & Metronome

### Short description (≤80 chars)
Tuner, metronome and chord/scale practice in one. Offline, one-time purchase.

### Full description

**The practice toolbox you actually open: tune, keep time, learn chords — and it remembers how long you practised.**

**■ Tuner: a steady needle that answers fast**
Play a string and the big dial shows flat or sharp to the cent; inside ±5 cents it turns green. Chromatic mode tunes any instrument; guitar, ukulele, violin and bass presets turn every string into a button and recognise which one you are playing. Reference A4 adjusts from 430 to 450 Hz.

**■ Metronome: scheduled by the audio engine, not a timer**
Beats are placed sample-accurately inside the audio stream, so 300 BPM is as steady as 60. Metres 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 and 6/8; quarter, eighth, triplet and sixteenth subdivisions; an accented downbeat and a beat light that follows the click. Tap tempo, long-press to nudge by ten. Keeps ticking with the screen locked; stops itself for a phone call.

**■ Chords and scales: see them, then play them right**
About 50 chord, scale and mode types on 12 roots, each with its notes and degrees drawn on a guitar or ukulele fretboard or a keyboard. Guitar and ukulele also get an auto-derived first-position shape.
Play and check opens the microphone: pluck string by string and it confirms each one and how many cents it was off; for a scale, play it ascending and it ticks note by note.
Random drill: ten questions a round — name the chord from its notes, pick the notes from the name, bonus for streaks.

**■ Practice log: how long, and how in tune**
Minutes in the tuner, metronome and practice are logged per day; the share of steady tuner readings within ±5 cents becomes a trend line; consecutive practice days light the streak flame. A minute a day counts.

**■ Fully offline, no network permission**
The app has no network permission at all — check it in your system settings. The microphone opens only for the tuner and play-and-check; audio is analysed on the phone in real time, never recorded or uploaded, and it closes the moment you leave the page or the app goes to the background. No account, no ads, no analytics SDK.

**Free includes**
· Chromatic tuner with adjustable A4
· Metronome in 2/4, 3/4, 4/4 with quarter and eighth subdivisions, tap tempo
· Five chord/scale types (major, minor, dominant 7th chords; major scale; minor pentatonic) with play-and-check and drills
· The last 7 days of your practice log

**Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription)**
· Guitar, ukulele, violin and bass presets with a button per string
· 6/8 metre, triplet and sixteenth subdivisions
· The full chord and scale dictionary (about 50 types × 12 roots)
· Unlimited practice history with 30-day trends
