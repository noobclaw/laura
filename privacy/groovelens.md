# Groovelens — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

Groovelens does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

- **Your audio stays on your iPhone.** Songs, videos and recordings you import are analyzed on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Microphone.** The microphone is used only when you record a song or play in Kit mode, to hear your drumming and score your timing. The audio is processed on the device and is not transmitted.
- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.
- **Purchases.** In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
- **Data on your device.** Your songs, charts and progress are stored in the app's private storage and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **Children.** The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Questions: https://256btc.com/support/

# Groovelens 隱私政策

_最後更新：2026 年 11 月 26 日_

Groovelens 不收集、不上傳、不分享也不出售任何個人資料。

- **音訊只留在你的 iPhone。** 你匯入的歌曲、影片和錄音都在本機分析，不會上傳。
- **麥克風。** 只有在錄音或使用真鼓模式時才會使用麥克風，用來聽你打鼓並為節奏打分。聲音只在本機處理，不會傳送。
- **沒有帳號、沒有追蹤、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告。** App 內沒有任何第三方廣告或分析 SDK。
- **購買。** App 內購買由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資料。
- **本機資料。** 你的歌曲、鼓譜和進度存放在 App 的私有空間，刪除 App 即一併刪除。
- **兒童。** 本 App 不向任何人（包括兒童）收集資料。

聯絡我們：https://256btc.com/support/
