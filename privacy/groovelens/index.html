Privacy Policy

Groovelens

Drum transcription and rhythm practice, on device
Effective date: 2026-11-26

Groovelens collects nothing about you. There is no account and no server. Songs, videos and recordings you import are analyzed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information: no name, email, phone number, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage analytics or crash logs.

Permissions this app asks for

  • Microphone — only when you record a song to chart, or play in Kit mode so the app can hear your drumming and score your timing. The audio is processed on your device and never transmitted.

Choosing a video from Photos uses the system picker, which gives the app only the video you pick. No photo-library permission is requested.

Where your data lives

Your songs, drum charts, practice history and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data. Separated drum tracks are excluded from iCloud backup because the app can regenerate them.

Third parties

Groovelens contains no third-party SDKs — no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no social logins.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. We only learn whether Pro is unlocked and never see your payment details; see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Groovelens does not knowingly collect information from children — it collects information from no one.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. Delete songs inside the app, or delete the app to erase everything.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions: bitcexgroup@gmail.com · 256btc.com/support
