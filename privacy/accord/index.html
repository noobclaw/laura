Privacy Policy

Accord

Perfume formula lab, on your iPhone
Effective date: 2026-11-26

Accord collects nothing about you. There is no account and no server. Your materials, formulas, batches and notes are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information: no name, email, phone number, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, usage analytics or crash logs.

Permissions this app asks for

  • Notifications — only when you save a batch, so Accord can remind you on the days you planned to smell it. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Face ID — only if you turn on App Lock, to unlock your formula book. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data. Files leave your iPhone only when you export or back them up yourself through the share sheet.

Third parties

Accord contains no third-party SDKs — no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no social logins.

Purchases are handled by the App Store. We only learn whether Pro is unlocked and never see your payment details; see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Accord does not knowingly collect information from children — it collects information from no one.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. Delete formulas inside the app, or delete the app to erase everything.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s behaviour changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions: bitcexgroup@gmail.com · 256btc.com/support
