## Description
Start with a tiny corner shop and grow it into a supermarket empire.

Shoppers drive in, fill their baskets and queue at your tills. Every sale pays for the next upgrade: open new departments, hire staff, add checkouts and watch the money roll in, even while you are away.

BUILD YOUR STORE
• 11 departments, from Fresh Produce and Bakery to Frozen Foods, Snacks and Health & Beauty
• Up to 8 checkouts to keep the queues moving
• A bigger car park brings more shoppers through the door

WATCH IT GROW
• Your shop changes as you earn stars: corner shop, neighbourhood market, supermarket, hypermarket and finally a marble-floored flagship
• Put your own name on the sign out front

RUN IT YOUR WAY
• Hire staff and speed up every aisle
• Unload delivery trucks, welcome VIP shoppers and bus tours
• Complete goals for coins and gems
• Earn while you are offline, then collect when you return

PLAY ANYWHERE
• Plays fully offline
• No ads
• Available in English, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and Traditional Chinese
