Common questions

I bought something but did not get it. Open Shop and tap Restore. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I changed phones. Is my store gone? Progress is saved on the device only, so it does not move to a new phone. One-time purchases come back with Restore.

Can I rename my store? Yes. Open Settings and tap Store name.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
