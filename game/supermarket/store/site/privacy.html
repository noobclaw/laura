Souq Tycoon

Privacy policy

Souq Tycoon · Effective 24 November 2026

The game collects no personal data. Nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code in the game.

What stays on your device

Your store progress, your store's name, settings (language, sound) and your coins and gems are saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Network use

The game runs offline. It contacts the App Store only to show prices, to complete a purchase you start and to restore purchases you ask to restore.

Children

Because the game collects no data, there is nothing collected from children either. Purchases can be limited with Screen Time and Ask to Buy on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
